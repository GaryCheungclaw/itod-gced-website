ASD Awareness

Autism is not a disease and not a personality flaw — it's a different way of experiencing the world.

"Autism is not a disease, not a personality flaw — just a different way of experiencing the world."

Four Ways to Be a Better Friend

Simple principles we practice in every interaction — at school, at partner visits, everywhere.

  • 💬

    Speak directly and clearly

    No sarcasm, no hidden meanings. Say what you mean — autistic people often take words literally.

  • 🕊️

    Respect personal space

    Quiet time is not being rude. Everyone needs space to recharge — especially in busy, loud environments.

  • 🔊

    Sensory overload is real

    Loud noises and crowds can actually be painful. Be patient if someone needs to step away or cover their ears.

  • 🌊

    Meltdowns are not tantrums

    They are neurological overload, not bad behaviour. Stay calm, reduce stimulation, and don't judge.

Common Misconceptions

Three myths we hear most often — and the facts behind them.

❌ Myth: Autistic people don't have feelings.
✅ Fact: They feel deeply — they just express them differently.
❌ Myth: All autistic people are good at math.
✅ Fact: Autism is a spectrum — everyone is completely different.
❌ Myth: They don't want friends.
✅ Fact: They want connection too — socializing is just harder for them.
