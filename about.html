About Us

Who we are, who we work with, and why "Indifferent to Difference" is our name.

Our Name

Indifferent to Difference — we don't ignore difference, and we don't treat it as a flaw. We accept it equally and walk alongside each other.

Autism is not a disease and not a personality flaw — it is a different way of experiencing the world. Our club exists to turn that understanding into action.

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To raise autism awareness and support local ASD communities through hands-on service and campus advocacy.

🌟 Our Vision

A campus where difference is respected, and every individual is included.

Our Partners

We work with local organizations that support autistic youth in Guangzhou.

Guangdong Lingnan Vocational & Technical College
Our partner school for off-campus inclusive activities — cooking, sports and games with autistic students.
Lingnan Education Charity Foundation
Foundation partner supporting inclusive education and vocational programs for special-needs youth.
🎓
Xin Vocational Training (心职训)
Vocational support program helping autistic youth develop employment skills and independence.

Our Journey

  1. Established
    The Beginning
    Itod GCED was founded at AISG, launching regular visits to the autism center Rong Ai Zhi Jia (融爱之家) and partner schools.
  2. Growth
    Campus Fundraising & Awareness
    Added on-campus charity booths (Family Fun Day, Holiday Bazaar) and advocacy events like ImpactGZ and International Day.
  3. 2026–2027
    This Year
    Launching our official website, expanding vocational-support partnerships, and growing our team with elected officers.
